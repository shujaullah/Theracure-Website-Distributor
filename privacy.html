Privacy Notice

How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

Therapeutic Pharma & Distributor respects your privacy. This notice explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry or contact us, we may receive:

  • Your name and company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • The type of inquiry and the content of your message

Like most websites, we may also collect basic, anonymous usage data (such as pages visited and device type) through analytics tools to help us improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss business opportunities
  • To communicate with you about our services and partnerships
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing your information

We share your information only where necessary to respond to your inquiry, with service providers who help us operate this website, or where required by law.

Third-party services

This website uses third-party services including Google Maps, WhatsApp and website analytics. These services may collect information in line with their own privacy policies.

Your choices
