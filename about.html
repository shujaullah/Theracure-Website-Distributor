Who We Are

Company background

Therapeutic Pharma & Distributor is a Karachi-based pharmaceutical distribution business focused on developing professional relationships with pharmaceutical manufacturers and healthcare businesses.

Operating from North Nazimabad, we work as a business-to-business distribution partner — helping principal companies reach healthcare businesses in Karachi, and helping those businesses access the products they need through a professional, responsive service.
